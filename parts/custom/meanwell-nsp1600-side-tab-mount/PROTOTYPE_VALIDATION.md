# Mean Well NSP-1600 side-tab mount — N2 prototype plan

**Proposed tests, not performed.** N2 is a stationary indoor side-mounted prototype. Digital geometry checks and a plate-only strength screen do not qualify the actual case threads, side contact, countersunk heads, FPE anchors, tightening/locking procedure, final panel or side-orientation output. Record the received PSU revision/serial, hardware lots, material certificate, measured geometry, fixtures, calibrated instruments, loads, displacement and temperatures. Correct each failure before repeating affected tests.

## 1. Actual interface and manufactured fit

With the supply disconnected and its cover intact, verify the selected original +X side. **Confirm a suitable manufacturer-provided rear M4 thread before fit release.** The manufacturer drawing identifies M4, but the STEP contains a rear opening without its thread, nut or insert. Record actual thread pitch, condition, available engagement and internal clearance at both stations. Do not remedy a mismatch by adding an insert, loose internal nut, longer screw or a drilled feature by default.

Measure the actual two-hole pattern against the 1:1 template: X+2.3/Y−5.8 and −257.8 mm, 252 mm pitch. The manufacturer's general ±0.5 mm tolerance exceeds what fixed countersinks can reliably absorb. Revise the named coordinates before final machining if needed; do not force-fit or elongate finished cone seats. Confirm the real side metal contacts, toe regions and clearances to plastic/vents. A nominal coplanar area is not proof of case contact strength.

Inspect the finished **6.00±0.05 mm plate**, 45 mm spine, 72 mm tab width, 44 mm tab stations, R4 roots and drawing flatness/parallelism. Check finish, burrs and contact cleanliness. Use received screws to verify full seat contact, no rocking and a head flush to 0.10 mm below datum A. Measure **3.75–4.35 mm actual projection** at both seats; Mean Well's **5.00 mm maximum** always governs. Verify the screws' complete threads engage the actual case features and reach clamp-up without internal obstruction. No universal minimum engagement is inferred from the simplified STEP.

Inspect the final main panel and four FPE anchors for pattern, projection, flushness, installation/cure acceptance and support. Fit the real washer/nut stacks: full nylon engagement and **≥1.00 mm complete thread beyond every nut**, versus 1.45 mm nominal. Check an actual 5.5 mm AF socket with OD≤10 mm, internal depth≥10 mm and 60 mm approach. No pad/TIM or fan-relief pockets belong to this design.

## 2. Qualify the contact and joints before proof loading

Obtain manufacturer/process acceptance or representative-joint evidence for the M4 screw, actual front/rear threads, finished countersink, side toe contact and locking method. Mean Well recommends **0.6865–0.9807 N·m** for side mounts; this does not establish a clamp force or qualify the custom head/seat. Record seating and local deformation at the approved tightening condition. Do not infer friction from appearance or add threadlocker without a compatible procedure.

The proposed **case-joint qualification demand is 1250 N tension and 175 N shear per M4**, with **1070 N toe compression** in the paired case. These are demands to be substantiated, not allowables or successful tests. Toe compression is part of the same balanced screw/contact case, not another external body force. Qualify the associated metal toe bearing/contact path: two collinear screws alone do not resist all roll directions. Do not test by pulling a screw while omitting the compression contact required by the engineering model. Resolve the separate countersink-seat pressure check with the received head and actual contact before joint release; the transverse-frame factor does not qualify this seat.

Use a case-safe metal surrogate reproducing both axes and the relevant metal support/toe regions for initial plate/contact load correlation. Use representative final-thickness FPE panel coupons for anchor qualification. No proof force should pass through a connector, terminal, fan, plastic feature or an unqualified real PSU boss. The proposed **FPE qualification envelope is 500 N tension and 250 N shear per stud**; this is not a supplier rating or proof that prying is bounded. Qualify combined loading, tightening/prevailing torque and temperature under the actual installation process before complete-assembly loading. The numerical cases are controlled by the final [engineering results](references/engineering/engineering_results.json).

## 3. Static retention and repeated service

Use the final [engineering note](ENGINEERING_NOTE.md) to prepare a signed fixture/load sheet, including its toe-contact and station-sharing assumptions. The working device envelope is **125 N resultant plus an independent 5 N·m couple**, representing gravity plus 3g handling and a 50 N cable force with 100 mm lever. The complete package allowance is **2.2 kg**, producing a **140 N package resultant** under the same basis; weigh the real package to confirm that allowance. The resultant direction changes between cases; do not apply full components on every axis simultaneously unless that stronger case is separately analyzed.

On surrogates first, apply both roll directions, reverse tension, longitudinal/transverse shear and the relevant combined cases. Include the adverse case in which one station carries the full roll moment. Record local tab/frame displacement and case-to-plate rotation, subtracting fixture compliance. A reviewed 1.5× plate proof scales the **entire coupled force/contact case**, including the cable couple to 7.5 N·m; do not multiply only one force in a balanced fixture. Hold each stable static plateau for 60 seconds, record unloading residuals, and inspect threads, cones, toe marks and stud anchors. Proposed acceptance is **≤0.30 mm local elastic movement, ≤0.40 mm combined with longitudinal self-load, and ≤0.10 mm residual movement**. Require no fracture, release, loosening or new plastic contact; loss or migration of the required toe contact is a failure even if the metal plate remains elastic. A plate-only surrogate proof does not qualify actual case or stud interfaces.

Only after all actual joints are qualified should a complete disconnected assembly be exposed to its approved handling case. Fit real cables, lugs, covers and strain relief; use cable-maker bend radii and verify tool access. Apply cable forces at a safe strain-relief fixture, not an unqualified terminal. Perform **ten unpowered removal/reinstallation cycles**, including ≥20 mm lift off the studs. Record seat/thread condition, witness-mark movement, contact stability and locknut performance at cycles 1, 5 and 10. Use the accepted final procedure for replacement nuts and locking.

## 4. Side-orientation thermal release

**No rated-load assumption is permitted from the horizontal ambient curve.** Confirm actual voltage variant, input range, duty/load, enclosure inlet ambient and the manufacturer's required side-orientation derating or a qualified assessment of the actual equipment. Preserve factory terminal-end intake and fan exhaust. The 100 mm end corridors are planning reserves; retain 100–150 mm separation from an adjacent heat source as the manufacturer directs. Panel contact provides no credited heat-rejection path.

Qualified electrical personnel should progress from a monitored moderate load toward the intended, appropriately derated limits. Log input/output power, PSU inlet/exhaust, enclosure air, panel/nuts, humidity and TEC hot-side behavior. A proposed steady-state criterion is <1°C change over 15 minutes after at least one hour at a test point; extend the test for drift or cycling. Check actual nut temperature against its **85°C catalog limit**, absence of harmful recirculation/condensation, fan operation and manufacturer electrical/thermal limits. Recheck contact, fastener witness marks and fit while warm and after cooling.

At the provisional 12 V full-output reference point, 1500 W output and 89% typical efficiency imply about **185 W PSU loss**. This is a planning reference, not permitted N2 output, measured heat or demonstrated TEC capacity. Include other internal losses and the complete enclosure heat-rejection path. Release only to the tested orientation/load/input/ambient and support conditions. Vehicle, airborne, outdoor, overhead, seismic, fatigue and vibration service remain unqualified; changed requirements require review and additional tests.
