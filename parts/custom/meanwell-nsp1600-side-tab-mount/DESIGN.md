## Concept and interfaces

1. **Selected: single machined side adapter with local ears.** Uses the designated M4 side interface, remains flush beneath the countersunk heads, permits one-piece removal and preserves both end airflow paths. Metal side contact and the screw tension couple must resist roll; a two-point force-only bolt-group calculation would be singular and is not acceptable.
2. A folded narrow tray using bottom holes would introduce a vertical flange, more bend/fastener access requirements and a wider foot; it may become appropriate if the actual side interface is unsuitable.
3. Rotating the full N1 adapter with an angle base would retain more parts, horizontal area and service obstruction. It does not meet the user's local-tab preference as directly.
