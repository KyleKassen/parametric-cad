# Mean Well NSP-1600 side-tab mount — N2 assembly

**Proposed instructions; no physical assembly or test was performed.** Use the N2 drawings, [BOM](BOM.md) and [prototype plan](PROTOTYPE_VALIDATION.md). The supply is placed on its original +X narrow side, with an 85 mm body height above the adapter. This is a stationary indoor mechanical prototype; allowable output in this orientation is not established.

## Receiving and joint gates

1. Disconnect and secure the system against energization. Preserve the cover, original hardware and FG protective-earth provision. Support the case safely; do not rest or load it through terminals, fan frames or exposed features.
2. **Confirm both actual side M4 mounting threads.** The front source geometry shows a short boss; the rear contains only an opening without its thread/nut/insert, although the manufacturer drawing identifies M4. Confirm received variant, pitch, engagement, contact geometry and internal clearance. If the rear has no suitable manufacturer-provided thread, this design cannot be installed as drawn. Do not add an insert, nut or longer screw or modify the supply by default.
3. Measure both side axes against the 1:1 template: X+2.3/Y−5.8 and −257.8 mm. The manufacturer general ±0.5 mm tolerance cannot be guaranteed by fixed countersinks. Revise named hole coordinates before final machining if necessary; never draw a misaligned part into position using screw torque.
4. Inspect the finished 6.00±0.05 mm adapter: 45 mm spine, 72 mm local width, 44 mm tab stations, R4 contour, clean external edge breaks, datum A flatness and top-to-A parallelism per drawing. The full contact planes must be free of raised burrs, chips or finish buildup. No fan pockets or TIM are selected. Confirm actual metal contact at the engineering-defined toe regions; a rocking or unsupported case invalidates the roll-load assumption.
5. Gauge each received M4×10 screw in its own finished seat. Require full conical seating without rocking, head flush to 0.10 mm below A, and **3.75–4.35 mm measured projection** from the upper seating face. **Never exceed Mean Well's 5.00 mm maximum.** Confirm actual thread traversal and unobstructed clamp-up. The catalog head's 2.3 mm overall height is not completely represented by the ideal CAD cone.

## Bench assembly and installation

1. Place the adapter against the selected narrow side on a case-safe bench fixture. Start both [91294A190 screws](https://www.mcmaster.com/91294A190/) by hand without head washers. Keep the opposite-side and cover hardware unchanged.
2. Tighten evenly using a qualified joint procedure. Mean Well's side-mount recommendation is **0.6865–0.9807 N·m**; it does not alone validate the selected head, aluminum seat, case toe, finish or locking treatment. Resolve that compatibility before production tightening. No alternate torque or threadlocker is specified. Verify metal seating and contact distribution without crushing the case. The two screw stations and unilateral side contact together resist roll; screw spacing alone does not establish this capacity.
3. Inspect four factory-installed FPE WGU30 M3×12 load studs at X±28.5/Y−5.8 and −257.8 mm. Confirm actual pattern, projection, flush base, cure/installation acceptance and final panel support. The depicted 92×340×6 mm panel is only an interface reference. Do not grind installed studs or place a pad beneath the adapter.
4. Lower the complete unit/adapter over the studs without binding. Fit one [98688A142 washer](https://www.mcmaster.com/98688A142/) and [90576A102 locknut](https://www.mcmaster.com/90576A102/) to each. Require full nylon engagement and **≥1.00 mm of complete stud thread beyond each nut**, compared with 1.45 mm nominal. Check the real 5.5 mm AF socket: OD≤10 mm, internal depth≥10 mm and 60 mm axial approach. Use the qualified FPE tightening/locking procedure; nut prevailing torque must not damage the anchor. Add witness marks after accepted tightening.
5. Confirm cable/lug/cover, CN1/CN2, adjustment, FG, strain-relief and tool access. Keep cables and adjacent equipment out of the intake/exhaust path. The adapter provides no credited cooling function. Side-orientation derating and actual enclosure operation must pass the prototype plan before operational release.

## Removal

Disconnect and secure power, disconnect/support cables, and support the tall unit while removing four nuts and washers. Lift the complete supply/adapter at least 20 mm to clear the studs. Remove the two underside M4 screws only on the bench. Inspect contact marks, finish, seats, case threads, stud anchors and witness marks before reinstalling. Complete the proposed ten unpowered service cycles; assess or replace locknuts under the qualified procedure. Their 85°C catalog limit applies to the actual nut temperature.
