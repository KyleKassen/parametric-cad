# Adapter material, finish and provisional screw locking

Research date: 2026-09-08. This records source-backed material inputs and proposed production requirements. No stock certificate, coating certificate, installation torque, chemical compatibility test or loosening test has been obtained.

## Material and machining

Specify certified flat-rolled **6061-T6**, or **6061-T651** if available in the chosen stock form; nominal 4 mm starting stock, machined to **3.50 ±0.05 mm after finish**. Confirm stock availability and enough cleanup allowance for finished flatness. Do not substitute annealed, T4, cast tooling plate or an unspecified aluminum alloy. The material certificate must identify alloy, temper and applicable product specification, and substantiate the design minimum yield requirement.

The [thyssenkrupp EN AW-6061 material datasheet](https://ucpcdn.thyssenkrupp.com/_legacy/UCPthyssenkruppBAMXUK/assets.files/material-data-sheets/aluminium/aluminium-6061.pdf), p2, gives room-temperature sheet/strip/plate properties for **T6/T62/T651, thickness >3.0–6.0 mm**: 0.2% proof/yield strength **≥240 MPa**, tensile strength **≥290 MPa**, elongation A50 **≥10%**. Both proposed starting and finished thicknesses fall in that interval. Its p3 elastic modulus is **70 GPa, guidance only**; p5 cites DIN EN485-2:2016-10. This is source support for an engineering requirement, not certification of purchased stock.

The calculation value **E=69 GPa is a selected approximate stiffness input, not a guaranteed minimum**. As a sensitivity check, the [Kaiser 6061 sheet/coil/plate datasheet](https://online.kaiseraluminum.com/depot/PublicProductInformation/Document/1015/Kaiser_Aluminum_6061_Sheet_Coil_and_Plate.pdf) lists typical 10.0 Msi alongside 68.3 GPa; those two printed numbers are not exact unit conversions. Using 68.3 instead of 69 increases calculated elastic movement by about **1.03%**. Neither source establishes a minimum modulus or a tested finished-part stiffness.

Manufacturing requirements selected for this design: machine both faces as necessary with supported workholding; inspect the released part for flatness after unclamping. Deburr before finishing, preserve countersink seating geometry, and verify thickness and actual screw-head recession after finishing. Do not weld, anneal or apply an unreviewed thermal treatment. No forming or bend-property claim is needed for this flat machined part.

## Finish

Drawing callout: **CHEMICAL CONVERSION COAT PER MIL-DTL-5541, TYPE II, CLASS 3; CLEAR/NATURAL APPEARANCE. APPLY AFTER MACHINING AND DEBURRING. FINISHED DIMENSIONS APPLY.** Use a finisher competent for this process and request a certificate identifying the process, type/class and qualified conversion material. Clear appearance is a drawing requirement; slight process color variation is not an established rejection limit.

[DLA ASSIST's current MIL-DTL-5541 record](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/qsDocDetails.aspx?ident_number=5499) identifies active Revision F with the 2024 validation notice. An official DLA equipment specification, [A–A–59832D §3.6.4.2](https://quicksearch.dla.mil/Transient/063B771CF6B04388A8CF295B96ED1265.pdf), expressly uses clear Type II/Class3 conversion coating for low electrical resistance. [DLA's current MIL-DTL-81706 qualification entry](https://qpldocs.dla.mil/search/print_govt_mfg.aspx?govt=34155484) includes BONDERITE M-CR T5900 AERO for Type II/Class3, Form I, Method B. This demonstrates an available qualified material route; it does not qualify a particular finisher or this finished part.

The coating choice provides a practical specified conversion finish without a thick decorative layer at the countersinks. **No RF bond, grounding path, contact resistance or thermal improvement is guaranteed.** Existing radio finish, FPE panel finish, contamination, pressure and contact geometry still govern the assembled interface. Treat any required protective-earth or RF bonding function as a separate installation requirement and test it.

## Prototype screws versus a qualified locking process

Keep the four selected **McMaster 91294A126 M3×6 DIN7991 screws** for the first monitored fit prototype. First establish actual screw length, countersink recession and **2.4–2.9 mm maximum external penetration** at all four case inserts against the final drawing and the opposing internal PCB screws. This penetration range is a project limit, not an Ettus allowable specification. Establish a repeatable installation and removal torque on representative hardware before claiming a controlled preload; no torque value is supplied by this note.

For that initial prototype, use clean threads with **no added liquid threadlocker, primer, solvent flooding or added lubricant in the radio**. After the joint is seated using the agreed prototype assembly procedure, place removable, compatible witness marks from screw heads onto the adapter only. Photograph the marks; inspect before and after every handling/load/thermal trial and before each use. The heads are hidden once the adapter is on the FPE panel: remove the adapter/radio assembly from the panel studs to inspect them, following the unplugged service procedure. Stop if a mark moves or a joint gaps. Witness marks only reveal rotation; they do not prevent loosening. This is a monitored prototype approach, not a validated vibration-locking method.

**LOCTITE222 is a later low-strength candidate, not an approved application here.** [McMaster's official purple-threadlocker table](https://www.mcmaster.com/products/purple-loctite/) verifies **1810A27, LOCTITE222, 0.34 fl oz bottle**; an exact [product link](https://www.mcmaster.com/1810A27/) is provided for later procurement. No price was verified and no consumable is added to the purchase BOM.

The [Henkel LOCTITE222 TDS, May2022](https://datasheets.tdx.henkel.com/LOCTITE-222-en_GL.pdf), describes low-strength anaerobic curing between close-fitting metal threads and hand-tool removal. Cure depends on substrate, gap and temperature; it specifies clean, dry surfaces and activator SF7471/SF7649 when inactive material or slow cure requires it. It cautions about plastics compatibility. Its typical **6 N·m breakaway** result is for **M10 steel nuts/bolts after24h at22°C**. That is neither an M3 result nor an installation torque. Generic TDS through-hole application instructions must not be transferred to the radio without a controlled quantity and contamination review.

Before adopting222, qualify it on a representative insert/screw/finish joint: metered application that cannot reach electronics or the opposing PCB screw; compatible cleaning and cure procedure; completed cure at actual conditions; retention under the proposed loads; removal torque below verified insert spin-out/pullout and screw-head limits; and repeated service without debris or damage. Inspect the actual case materials and service restrictions. Do not use the TDS's exceptional heated-removal method on an assembled radio. Until these checks are complete, there is **no validated threadlocker or preload procedure** for the radio attachment.
