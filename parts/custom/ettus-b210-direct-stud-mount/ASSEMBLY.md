# Installation and removal - D1 prototype

Use the drawing with this sequence.
The radio attachment is a provisional interface until its four inserts and the FPE studs have qualified load and tightening limits.
No installation torque is assigned from appearance, screw size or catalog strength class.

## Before assembly

1. Disconnect power, USB, RF and reference cables.
Support the enclosure on a clean padded bench without loading connectors or exposed parts.
Remove the four adhesive feet and residue using an enclosure-compatible process; do not flood solvents into the holes.
Keep the feet for restoration or obtain equivalent replacements if their adhesive cannot be reused.
2. Check each M3 x0.5 thread with an appropriate gauge and inspect the entry and complete thread length.
The user estimated 4 mm depth; the STEP shows opposing internal screws only3.5738 mm inward.
Use a non-damaging depth/inspection method with power disconnected; do not probe the PCB or bottom a candidate screw experimentally.
Do not remove, shorten or loosen the original PCB screws to make this design fit.
3. Confirm the four insert end faces and pan seating surface are flat and clean.
Each insert must bear directly on the adapter; rocking, recessed insert contact, case distortion or a proud metal head requires measured redesign.
Do not use screw preload to pull a warped plate or pan into alignment.
4. Inspect the finished adapter thickness3.50 +/-0.05, hole positions and underside flatness.
Use received McMaster91294A126 screws to gauge each90-degree seat.
Require every head flush to0.10 mm below datum A, and actual straight bore ligament at least1.80 mm after all deburrs.
The reference mouth is6.1 mm; screen mouth<=6.2 and included angle89-91 degrees, with effective conical seating diameter>=5.70 mm, full conical contact and no rocking head.
Do not simply overcut a countersink to conceal incompatible hardware.

## Fit the adapter to the radio

5. Seat the four M3 x0.5 x6 mm overall-length screws in the plate before offering the assembly to the radio.
There are no washers beneath these countersunk heads.
Measure each protrusion beyond the radio-contact face; accept2.4-2.9 mm only if at least0.50 mm separation from the actual internal screw/obstruction and at least2.0 mm complete thread engagement remain after the entry and screw-tip incomplete threads.
This is an application acceptance proposal, not an Ettus-approved depth or capacity.
If both engagement and tip clearance cannot be obtained with the selected hardware, stop and revise the thickness/screw combination.
6. Align all four housing holes and start screws by hand without force or cross-threading.
Seat uniformly in a diagonal sequence under the qualified prototype assembly procedure, using a2 mm hex key or bit.
Verify direct seating and absence of case distortion.
For the monitored fit prototype use clean threads and compatible removable witness marks from each head to the adapter; marks indicate rotation and do not lock a screw.
Liquid threadlocker is not approved for this assembled radio; a later locking procedure must control migration, cure and removal torque.
7. Check the entire adapter underside against a flat witness surface.
No head, burr or debris may stand proud.
Photograph the screw witness marks before they become hidden by the supporting panel.

## Attach to the FPE panel

8. Inspect the factory-installed M3 x12 load studs at the136 x120.015 pattern.
Use the native FPE WGU30 load-stud variant, reverse side, zero depth offset; do not substitute plain bonded studs without review.
Stud bases and adhesive must be flush or below the main-panel seating surface.
Verify panel thickness, remaining pocket floor, anchor installation and approved tension/shear/torque limits.
The reference6 mm panel coupon does not define the finished enclosure's panel support.
9. Lower the radio/adapter straight over all four studs until the flat underside seats freely on the main panel.
Fit one McMaster98688A142 washer and one90576A102 nylon-insert locknut to each exposed stud.
Verify full nylon engagement and at least two complete threads beyond each nut.
The nominal thread tail is3.95 mm; inspect actual stud and nut dimensions.
10. Use a5.5 mm AF socket with outside diameter<=10 mm and clear internal depth>=9 mm to avoid bottoming on the protruding stud.
Check the actual tool body and any extension fit.
The layout reserves a40 mm vertical socket envelope starting at the plate top; include tool insertion access in the final box layout.
Tightening plus locknut prevailing torque must remain below the qualified FPE anchor limit; stop if a stud rotates, the panel dimples or the plate rocks.
11. Mate actual cables, provide their specified bend radii, and add strain relief on the supporting structure.
The CAD preserves110 mm-wide x60 mm-long x32 mm-high corridors at the connector ends; these are planning envelopes, not actual plug models or guaranteed cable bend space.
The assumed20 N cable force is a mount design input, not a connector allowable.
Verify operation and temperature before unattended use.

## Removal and service

Disconnect cables and support the radio.
Remove the four panel-stud nuts and washers, then lift the complete radio/adapter straight by at least13 mm to clear the12 mm studs.
Reserve at least20 mm additional vertical lift space in the final box and room to grasp the assembly.
The sampled CAD path is clear; actual cable and box geometry must also permit it.
The radio's housing screws remain installed during routine removal from the panel.
On the bench, inspect the hidden witness marks and four countersunk heads; remove those screws only when the adapter must be separated.
Replace worn or damaged screws and locknuts, and inspect the original inserts after repeated service.
Restore adhesive feet if returning the radio to unmounted use.
