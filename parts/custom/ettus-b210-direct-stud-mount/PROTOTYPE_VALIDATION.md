# D1 prototype validation plan

No step below has been physically performed.
Record radio serial/revision, received hardware lots, adapter material/finish certificates, measured values, photographs, instrumentation and pass/fail decisions.
Close the fit and joint-strength items before equipment service.

| Check | Method | Proposed acceptance / action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Configuration | Compare real case, connectors, pan, inserts and internal screws with supplied STEP; confirm M3 x0.5 with gauge | Match measured93.599 x120.015 axes; document deviations and regenerate if needed |
| Adapter dimensions | Inspect finished thickness, A flatness, locations, bores, radius, deburrs and finish | Drawing tolerances after finish; no burr, rocking or warped seating |
| Screw head and penetration | Measure received6 mm overall screws and seated head recession; measure actual obstructed depth at each insert using safe equipment | Heads flush to0.10 below A;2.4-2.9 protrusion only with>=0.5 actual tip gap and>=2.0 full engagement after incomplete-thread losses |
| Contact | Inspect all four metal insert end-face contacts without forcing the pan | Four direct contacts, no case distortion; reject gaps or pan-only clamping |
| FPE interface | Inspect native load-stud identity, projection, pattern, flush bases, adhesive, panel thickness and pocket floor | Actual pattern within agreed supplier tolerance; bases flush/below; no forced seating; adequate stud tail |
| Tightening and locking | Qualify representative joints and record installation/removal limits, prevailing torque, clamp force if measured, head seating and witness marks | No insert spin, pullout, stud movement or local crushing; physical procedure approved before operating installation; marks are inspection only |
| Actual mass/loads | Weigh radio and completed package; measure CG and cable routing | Radio<=1.0 kg and package<=1.3 kg, load height<=50 mm, or recalculate |
| Tools/cables/service | Use every intended plug, antenna lead, USB/DC/reference cable and actual tool; perform5 complete removal/reinstall cycles | Unforced mating, specified cable bends, no connector loads from support,5.5AF socket<=10OD and>=9depth fits;13 mm minimum lift available; no thread/seat damage |
| Thermal operation | Compare unmounted and mounted radio at same worst intended indoor ambient, duty cycle, actual box cooling and TX/RX settings; log ambient/case and available device telemetry until stable | Stay within manufacturer limits, no thermal faults/dropouts; investigate any mount-associated temperature rise>5 C as a proposed comparison trigger, not a manufacturer limit |
| Mount-only structure | Rigid four-point surrogate replaces radio; use representative adapter, panel/studs and actual supports; apply60 N device wrench +1 Nm in adverse directions and75 N package wrench as applicable | No loss of seating/retention, cracks or stud damage; correlate contact and motion with calculation before real-radio load testing |
| Mount-only proof | Only after joint limits are known, surrogate-load two same-row housing points to90 N each for60 s, unload and inspect | Proposed loaded-point movement<=0.5 mm, center<=0.8 mm, residual<=0.10 mm; no permanent damage or loosening. These are prototype criteria, not a completed qualification |
| Unknown anchors | Obtain FPE and case-insert allowables including preload/prying, or test representative joints with a qualified fixture | Preliminary FPE demand reserve150 N tension with75 N shear per stud is a demand to substantiate, not a catalog rating; no destructive proof on the real radio without separate authorization |
| Application qualification | Define real box installation, panel edge supports, orientation, shock/vibration spectrum, humidity/temperature, corrosion and grounding requirements | Review numerical requirements before vehicle, airborne, outdoor, overhead or sustained vibration use; current task provides none of those qualifications |

Inspect hidden screw witness marks by removing the radio/adapter from the studs before and after every prototype handling or thermal trial.
Stop on movement, gapping, cracking, contamination or abnormal device operation.
Revalidate screw depth and locking after any thickness, finish, hardware, case or mounting-pattern change.
