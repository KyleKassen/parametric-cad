## M4 screw

The exact [McMaster 91294A188](https://www.mcmaster.com/91294A188/) product page was read live: **M4×0.7×8 mm**, DIN 7991, 90° countersink, black-oxide alloy steel, head Ø8×2.3 mm, 2.5 mm hex, fully threaded 6g, Class 10.9 and 120,000 psi catalog tensile. Price **$6.25 per 100**, unit allocation $0.0625. Length is overall, measured from the top of the head. Six are needed for the six Tile axes.

For a hard-contact plate of thickness t and head recession r, nominal intrusion is **P=L+r−t**. Subtract actual separation only when a joint is not closed on hard stops: P=L+r−t−g. The selected 5.10 mm hard-contact plate, 8.00 mm screw and 0.05 mm head recession give **2.95 mm** nominal intrusion. The proposed actual acceptance is 2.85–3.00 mm, with ≥2.00 mm effective complete engagement after entry and tip incomplete threads, subject to the fresh geometry/engineering review and physical gauging. This is a project criterion, not a manufacturer allowable. Gauge each finished seat and screw, actual entry/tip leads, depth and obstruction clearance. The 2.3 mm catalog head height is not represented by an ideal Ø8→Ø4/90° cone alone; use received hardware for seat acceptance. Catalog class/tensile values do not prove reduced countersunk-head or female-thread capacity. No installation torque is inferred.

[Dow's appliance material guide](https://www.dow.com/documents/11/11-3930-01-silicones-from-dow-for-appliances.pdf?iframe=true) places 340 in the noncuring **thin bond-line thickness <100 µm** category. Its typical k is 0.7 W/mK. The manufacturer-authored [340 TDS mirrored by Conro](https://www.conro.com/es/downloads/Manuals/Dowsil-340-TDS.pdf) gives typical k 0.67 W/mK and impedance 0.16°C·cm²/W **at 40 psi**; retrieval was intermittent. That pressure-conditioned value must not be assigned to this unqualified low-preload joint. The older [Dow-hosted Portuguese 340 TDS](https://www.dow.com/documents/01/01-1641-11-dowsil-340-heat-sink-compound.pdf?iframe=true) describes grease-like, nonhardening behavior but lists 0.0010 cal/(cm·s·°C), equivalent to **0.4184 W/mK**. Engineering should include a 0.42 W/mK sensitivity and confirm the received lot's current TDS/SDS; do not mix older/regional formulations or data indiscriminately.

Tgel 600 supports fixed small gaps and easy rework, and lists low pump-out. Its quoted peak <48 N and residual <6 N lack coupon area/geometry in the datasheet; they cannot be scaled into full-face assembly force. Do not use dispensing pressure as interface pressure. Its resistance results are typical test data, not a complete Bedrock-to-panel thermal resistance.

## Process limits shared by both liquid candidates

Proposed construction: separate shallow TIM fields on both adapter faces, surrounded by integral hard metal islands that close the screw and stud clamp paths. Control finished gaps by geometry and inspected flatness. Meter only enough material to fill the fields; avoid screw bores, connectors, seams, ground contacts and excess squeeze-out. Do not use thick TIM to compensate for unmeasured rocking or raise screw torque to crush the chassis flat. Neither grease nor gel is the structural load path.

The final T1 geometry includes two 1 mm wide, 0.05 mm deep escape grooves per face at Y±20 mm, extending from the field to the +X edge. These provide a proposed escape path for air and excess grease; they do not prove complete filling or a safe closing force. The STEP controls their intersection with the outer chamfer. Pocket DXFs show the maximum planar floor footprint.

Assembly force remains unknown until a representative cold/warm coupon test shows field filling and hard-stop contact within safe screw/stud loads. For scale, 10 psi across 130×160 mm needs approximately **1.43 kN**; this is not a recommended preload. Surface flatness and verified area govern the actual bondline. For each service removal, inspect for dry spots, contamination and pump-out, clean using a method compatible with the actual coated surfaces, and reapply fresh material by the qualified procedure. No reuse-cycle life is established.

For bulk-only screening use R=t/(kA), with SI units and actual filled area. Include both interfaces, plate spreading, coatings/contact effects and the panel-to-ambient path in the system model. Neither high k nor an enclosure's 60W name proves system cooling capacity. Thermal and retention tests must run under representative electrical workload and ambient after final assembly.
