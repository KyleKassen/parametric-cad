# T1 prototype validation plan

No test below has been performed. Use a restrained bench setup with a catch/support and a sacrificial or dummy load before risking the computer. Record specimen serial numbers, material certificates, drawing revision, instruments and uncertainty.

| Stage | Method | Proposed acceptance / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Configuration | Confirm actual Tile/one-heatsink unit and official assembly; compare the six axes and flat face | No dependence on an unverified removable fin bank; actual coordinates agree with machining allowances |
| Threads and screw seats | Gauge M4 pitch, usable full-diameter depth and all six received screw heads/tips; measure assembled projection and complete engagement | All six pass ASSEMBLY.md simultaneously; no bottoming or internal contact; obtain permitted preload/torque and thread capacity |
| Flatness and contact | Measure plate/panel/Tile flatness; dry contact map; inspect recess depths; inspect thin-film coverage on a witness assembly | Hard lands seat without distortion; filled bond line >0 and <0.10 mm over intended active regions; no voids, trapped excess or dry high spots |
| Machining fit | Reopen drawing/STEP coordinates; install plate on actual FPE stud coupon and computer by hand | No forced alignment; no interference, proud screw head, damaged finish or case distortion |
| Tool and cable access | Use actual socket, hex key, SMA/RJ45/SFP/USB/DC cables, boots and mating tools | Full insertion, latch release and specified cable bend radii; no load through added hardware or fins; strain relief installed |
| FPE anchoring | Obtain supplier combined-load/torque/temperature limits or test representative installed studs in the actual panel material/process | Compare to calculated service demands and required safety margin; no pullout, panel tear-out, stud rotation or permanent set. Geometry alone is insufficient |
| Static retention | Progressively apply the engineering note's gravity, 20 N cable force at 50 mm lever and proof load cases with recorded force/moment | No slip beyond initial clearance take-up, screw loosening, case deformation, cracking or permanent displacement. Establish allowable displacement before applying load; recheck thermal contact afterward |
| Long-duration operation | Operate at intended workload and power settings, at maximum design inside-box temperature; log DC input W, Tile/adapter/panel/nut temperatures, CPU/SSD/NIC readings and TEC conditions | Within actual component and TIM/hardware limits with an agreed margin; no throttling attributable to cooling, shutdown or rising nonsteady temperature; stable filled thermal interfaces |
| Thermal path | Screen 10/30/60 W with controlled heat input or measured workloads; log fins/air/panel/TEC cold and hot sides; compare with baseline unit | Measured total heat-rejection performance meets defined conditions; do not treat the calculated bulk TIM drop as a system thermal rating |
| TEC integration | Measure airflow across the plate/fins or actual cold-side conductive joint, TEC Qc at operating ΔT, hot-side rejection and other enclosure loads | Qc exceeds combined loads at real conditions with margin; hot side rejects Qh=Qc+electrical input; setpoint/condensation control validated |
| Thermal cycling / creep | Cycle the intended cold/hot conditions and include prolonged maximum-temperature dwell; inspect at recorded intervals | No grease pump-out/void growth, fastener relaxation, corrosion, galvanic damage or loss of flat contact; nylon remains within rating |
| Service endurance | Perform at least 10 disconnect/lift/clean/repaste/reinstall cycles as an initial engineering trial | No stripped threads, bent studs, unsafe handling, progressive poor fit or loss of locking action; revise service process if needed |

Before thermal acceptance, establish maximum complete-computer electrical input, inside-box air temperature, continuous duty, installed TEC model/control setpoint and allowable component temperatures. The current user answer identifies an enclosure TEC but does not supply those numbers. If its cold surface can be below the enclosure dew point, include humidity, drainage/sealing strategy and controls in the test; this is a TEC integration issue, not solved by the adapter.

Before any vehicle, airborne, outdoor, overhead or significant vibration use, replace the stationary assumptions with application-specific acceleration spectra, duration, shock pulses, mounting orientation and inspection criteria. Repeat retention, loosening and thermal tests using those requirements. The present analytical 4 g handling envelope is not certification to a shock or vibration standard.
