# B210 external cradle — preliminary engineering note

Revision P1 is a manufacturable **prototype design for stationary indoor service**, with compact flat and upright arrangements. It is not an application-qualified product. The design uses a shared machined aluminum cradle with removable, non-clamping top bars; the upright arrangement adds two machined L-supports. The device remains unmodified and no mounting screw enters its case. The complete installation dimensions, support stiffness, mass, cable assemblies, operating temperature, shock/vibration exposure and removal frequency still require confirmation. The prototype plan defines the measurements and tests needed before release.

## Configuration and measured geometry

The supplied original STEP was directly imported with CadQuery/OpenCascade and preserved. The imported assembly contains **196 valid solids and 7,825 faces**, including enclosure pan/cover, PCB/components, separate connector panels, adhesive-foot geometry, seven coaxial connectors, USB 3.0 Type-B geometry, a DC socket, case screws and overlays. It is an enclosed device, not a bare board. ## Concept decision and load path

| Concept | Assessment |
|---|---|
| Ears/plate using device fixing points | Few parts and compact, but the physical external thread identity, useful engagement, safe penetration and pan/standoff capacity are not established. CAD-bore clearance is not an approved screw penetration. Not selected. |
| External cradle with removable retainers | Selected: a direct load path through distributed pan supports and positive stops, no device modification, straightforward removal, and open connector faces. Thin case contact and tolerance/escape geometry still need physical checks. |
| Formed tray or printed cradle | Plausible for a later process-specific design. A thin formed upright member adds bend/tooling and stiffness requirements. A printed version needs material, temperature, layer-direction, sustained-load and production-process validation. No such process qualification is available for this installation, so neither route is asserted interchangeable with the machined design. |

The shared base is machined 6061-T6/T651, 160 × 180 × 12 mm, with a relieved central pocket, 80 × 100 mm through-window, four Ø16 foot clearances and four integral 16 × 20 mm support lands. Rounded exterior corners, aligned hardware, 0.5 mm accessible edge breaks and a consistent black finish provide the equipment appearance without adding mechanisms. Two identical 160 × 20 × 8 mm top bars stand on four identical Ø12 × 30 mm spacers. Thin 0.20 mm total-thickness polyester films protect the intended contact areas; aluminum lands and retainers provide the structural capture. There are four structural part types including the upright adapter, with repeated bars/spacers/supports.

The top film underside is nominally 0.40 mm above the device. Adjust the finished spacer/shim stack to obtain **0.20–0.40 mm measured clearance**, cold and warm. Tightening the screws seats the bars on spacers, not on the cover. Gravity passes from the pan through lands and base to the support plate in the flat orientation. Side/end motion is limited by the pocket walls and low pan stops. Upward motion is captured by the bars; the curved lower pan end can develop an upward reaction, so bar overlap and lift limits are part of retention rather than friction assumptions. Opposed contacts and spaced bars restrain rotation. No friction coefficient or clamping preload is credited for retention.

The upright adapter pair has an 8 mm web/foot, 36 mm finished width and R6 internal root, profiled from certified 6061 plate; a suggested sawn blank is 75 × 180 × 38.1 mm before finishing. The cradle rotates +90° about Y and translates 96 mm upward. Nominal structural footprint is **73 × 180 mm**, height **176 mm**; hardware projections and cable space are additional. Adapter web holes are at Z = 26/166 mm and foot holes at X = 20/55 mm. Loads travel from the shared cradle through the web bolts, adapter roots and feet, then through bolts into the flat support. Install the feet first because the cradle limits access. No DIN-rail interface was selected; there is no verified rail requirement. Prior maritime project notes are not requirements for this design.

## Loads, properties and calculations

Inputs not supplied by the user are conservative design assumptions, not measured radio properties:

| Input/case | Value and use |
|---|---|
| Device mass allowance | 1.00 kg; weigh actual configured radio before release |
| Upright complete package allowance | 2.20 kg including device and mount; calculated mount mass is approximately 1.115 kg, so 1 kg radio + mount is approximately 2.115 kg; weigh and confirm actual mass/CG |
| Normal use | 1g gravity plus 20 N cable disturbance acting at 50 mm, equivalent moment 1.0 N·m |
| Device handling | Conservative 3g inertia + 1g gravity: 39.24 N; with co-directed cable force 59.24 N, rounded to 60 N |
| Upright handling | 2.2 kg × 4g + 20 N = 106.33 N, rounded to 110 N horizontal and 110 N vertical concurrently plus cable moment; concurrent maxima intentionally exceed a single acceleration vector |
| Structural screening/proof envelope | 150 N on one local bar/land/lip/rail; upright 150 N horizontal plus 150 N vertical and 1.5 N·m simultaneously, with CG height ≤120 mm and offset ≤50 mm from the upright web datum |

Use minimum yield **240 MPa** and elastic modulus **70 GPa** for certified, unwelded 6061-T6/T651 stock in the applicable plate/bar range. These are mount material inputs, not enclosure strength. Density 2,700 kg/m³ supports calculated mount mass. Manufacturer/supplier references and stock-form limits are in [the evidence register](references/research_sources.md). M5 coarse-thread tensile area is 14.2 mm²; A2-70 reference tensile/proof strengths are 700/450 MPa, subject to purchased-property-class certification. [6061 data sheet](https://d2zo35mdb530wx.cloudfront.net/_legacy/UCPthyssenkruppBAMXUK/assets.files/material-data-sheets/aluminium/aluminium-6061.pdf), [NBK fastener areas](https://static.nbk1560.com/en-US/resources/other/article/technical-29-mechanical-properties-of-fasteners-made-of-carbon-steel-and-alloy-steel/)

The analytical target is **yield safety factor ≥3** for the fabricated metal under the stated conservative screening loads. This reflects uncertain handling direction, local simplifications and a one-off prototype; it does not turn unverified case contacts into rated supports. The checks use elementary elastic beam/joint models: bending stress M/Z, beam deflection FL³/(48EI) or the applicable cantilever expression, threaded tensile area, bearing F/(td), net section and tear-out areas, and a conservative female-thread shear-area screen. Geometric amplification and local section reductions are identified in the detailed calculation report. No FEA was performed; the dominant outstanding uncertainty is the thin device case and physical capture/contact, for which an uncalibrated mesh would not supply an allowable load.

The reproducible calculation source and detailed results are [engineering_calculations.py](references/engineering/engineering_calculations.py), [engineering_checks.md](references/engineering/engineering_checks.md), and [engineering_results.json](references/engineering/engineering_results.json). The upright results include adverse finished dimensions: 35.8 × 7.95 mm sections, Ø5.6 mm holes, and 0.15 mm hole-position shifts. Principal results are:

| Check | Result and limitation |
|---|---|
| 8 mm retainer bar, 150 N central load over 140 mm | 25.90 MPa; 0.151 mm deflection; yield SF 9.26 |
| Local 6 mm floor strip, 150 N | 29.86 MPa; 0.022 mm deflection; yield SF 8.04 |
| Upright R6 root, amplified package load | 53.96 MPa equivalent stress with local factor; yield SF 4.45 |
| Lower upright web-hole net section | 79.62 MPa including factor 2; **minimum yield SF 3.01** |
| Upright foot-bolt demand | 346 N axial bound; screened at 400 N axial + 150 N shear per bolt; external proof SF 13.4 |
| M5 female-thread surrogate, ≥8 mm full engagement | 1,161 N allowable at SF3 under the specified shear-area model; installation preload can dominate and is not automatically acceptable |
| Upright movement | 0.449 mm normal operation; 1.435 mm handling; 1.957 mm proof upper-attachment estimate including foot rotation; bolt/support compliance remains unmodeled |
| End capture | 2.10 mm initial overlap minus 1.506 mm pitch/film-loss/bar/floor/rail-flex rise leaves 0.594 mm; after 0.20 mm dimensional reserve, **0.394 mm provisional margin**; bounded exact-contour checks pass, while physical capture remains unvalidated |

The narrow upright strength margin requires **7.95 mm minimum finished web/foot thickness**; a general ±0.20 mm tolerance is not acceptable on these sections. The end retaining edge requires ≤0.10 mm edge break rather than the general 0.50 mm. The proof deflection is a usable-clearance concern even when stress passes; reserve at least 2 mm moving-assembly space beyond measured connector/cable/tool needs, and enforce the prototype plan's service/proof movement limits. Installation preload is distinct from external service load; a low external thread load does not establish a safe tightening torque. The supporting plate, its own attachment to the installation, and enclosure local crushing remain conditional interfaces. These are analytical results, not physical validation.

## Manufacturing, fit and hardware

The base is a two-sided CNC part from plate: face to the specified finished thickness, machine pocket/label reliefs with a nominal Ø6 cutter and corner reliefs, cut the window/foot openings, machine lands, then drill/tap the mounting and retainer holes. Use sacrificial backing/fixture support and retained clamping stock during the through cuts; finish the second face and deburr. Bars are profile-machined from plate; spacers are turned/drilled or procured to the finished dimensional specification. Profile the L-supports from plate finished to 36 mm width with a practical roughing/finishing tool, preserving the R6 root; drill web and foot holes in accessible secondary setups. Plan sufficient flute/reach and rigid workholding for the thick profile. No undercut, weld, bend allowance or sheet-metal flat pattern is required for the selected geometry. DXF profiles are machining reference outlines, not certified laser-only fabrication instructions.

Specify certified 6061-T6/T651 unwelded stock, deburring, and nominal 10–15 µm black sulfuric anodizing; drawing dimensions apply after finish. Mask threads and bearing seats as specified. Obtain a finish sample if consistent appearance across mixed stock matters. Do not treat anodize or thin film as a certified electrical insulator, earth bond, corrosion qualification, or heat spreader. Grounding requirements need a deliberate approved bonding arrangement; no device modification or grounding credit is assumed.

M5 hardware remains distinct in the CAD assembly. Retainers use M5 × 50 ISO 4762 A2-70 screws and ISO 7089 washers through 8 mm bars and 30 mm spacers into M5 × 0.8-6H through threads in the 12 mm base. Nominal thread insertion is 50 − 1 − 8 − 30 = **11 mm**; account for screw, washer, spacer, bar, finish and thread-lead tolerances to meet the drawing's minimum effective engagement and avoid protrusion below the mounting face. Helical threads are represented by nominal/minor bores, not modeled thread flanks. Flat support attachment uses M5 × 30 through bolts; upright web joints use M5 × 30, and upright feet use M5 × 25 for the assumed 6 mm plate, with washers and the specified locknuts. Verify purchased locknut height and thread protrusion; select lengths again if plate thickness changes. The BOM/drawings govern the complete stack and locking provisions.

External loads are resisted by positive geometry and bolt bearing rather than assumed joint friction. Fastener tightening must seat metal-to-metal supports and preserve top clearance. No assembly torque has been invented: validate a controlled tightening/preload procedure for the actual screw finish, locking product and aluminum thread strength; supplier recommendations and representative joint testing are required. Repeated removal also requires inspection and replacement of locking consumables under their supplier's instructions.

LOCTITE 243 requires representative-joint cure and hand-tool removal qualification. Its typical adhesive breakaway values are not tightening torques. Actual stainless/aluminum surface condition and temperature govern the cure procedure; see the [Henkel evidence and application guidance](references/research_sources.md#hardware-data-and-precautions).

Worst-case fit must include actual pan/cover dimensions, case coatings, machined pocket tolerance, finished land height, spacer/bar tolerance, film total thickness and loss, fastener play and elastic deflection. A 0.40 mm nominal top gap is not a fit validation. The lower pan is curved: after lift it can shift farther toward a low end stop, and sufficiently large lift could climb the stop. The geometry checks and analytical retention margin are screening only; confirm the escape envelope physically at maximum gaps, film-loss condition, and allowable movement. If sufficient remaining overlap cannot be demonstrated, revise the stops/retainers before use.

The [explicit tolerance stack](references/fit_tolerance_stack.md) gives a raw top-gap range of **−0.11 to +0.91 mm** from the nominal 0.40 mm gap and summed ±0.51 mm adverse allowances, including assumed device variation. This requires measured spacer selection and shimming; P1 is not an interchangeable drop-in fit. Select the spacer/under-bar stack to achieve **0.20–0.40 mm at every station, cold and warm**. Separate under-head annular shims adjust screw entry and tip recess without changing the top gap. Verify ≥8 mm effective full thread engagement and ≥0.50 mm tip recess after both selections. The specified gap is a physical assembly acceptance condition, not a claim that nominal production tolerances alone achieve it.

## Access, completed verification and release limits

The bars sit away from the top dimples and leave most external surfaces open. The center window preserves access/airspace below the case; it is not a demonstrated cooling improvement. Both connector faces and K-Slots require clearance. CAD service envelopes must cover actual plugs, mating motion, SMA tools and cable bend radii; manufacturer interface names alone do not supply those dimensions. Use external cable strain relief on the support and test with the intended cable assemblies. Unplug cables and remove both bars before radio removal; hold/support the upright radio while opening the capture. Case service occurs after removal.

Completed source checks include direct STEP import, explicit units, source identity, solid validity, bbox/face/slot measurement, curved-pan sections and multiple inspected views. The final [geometry/export verification report](exports/verification_report.json) records **34 passing checks** covering manufactured-part validity and millimetre scale on reopen, nominal device/mount clearance, assumed connector corridors, sampled straight insertion/removal, tool access, screw stack and bounded retention. The reopened flat and upright STEP assemblies contain 235 and 253 valid solids respectively, with device, mounting parts and hardware distinct. The [hardware verification report](exports/hardware_verification.json) records **18 passing checks**, including nominal hardware/device interference and foot/web fastening tool corridors.

Both [pan-only end-stop capture witnesses](references/geometry/pan_capture_check.json) also pass: at the bounded extreme tilt/lift and attempted outward displacement, each low stop obstructs the metal pan itself. These are static geometric witnesses, not a continuous six-degree-of-freedom escape search, impact simulation, contact-strength result or physical retention test. Connector checks use assumed service envelopes rather than purchased cable models. No physical prototype, tested fit, proven enclosure load capacity, fatigue life, thermal improvement or installation qualification is claimed.

[The prototype validation plan](prototype_validation_plan.md) specifies receiving inspection, measured cold/warm gaps and contact maps, surrogate-only mount proof, staged case-safe actual-radio loading, cable access, thermal comparison under representative simultaneous TX/RX, and ten unplugged removal/reinstallation cycles. The main unresolved items are actual device configuration/mass/CG and dimensional variation; pan/cover local load capacity and low-stop capture; actual plugs/bend radii; torque/preload/locking behavior; support plate and installation attachment; and operating temperature/airflow. Record those results and release only the tested configuration and load/environment envelope.
