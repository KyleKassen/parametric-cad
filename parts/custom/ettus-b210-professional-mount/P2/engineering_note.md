# P2 engineering note — thinner machined B210 mount

P2 develops the user's request for thinner plates and McMaster hardware while retaining both flat and upright options. It remains a preliminary design for a rigid supporting plate inside a stationary indoor equipment box. Installation space, enclosure ventilation, actual plate stiffness/attachment, environment, removal frequency and vibration exposure are not confirmed. The supporting plate's 6 mm thickness is a hardware-stack assumption, not a structural rating.

## Concept and consequential changes

| Concept | Decision |
|---|---|
| Ears using existing case fasteners | Compact, but thread identity, penetration and case load capacity remain unverified. |
| External cradle with removable bars | Selected: positive capture and direct pan-to-frame load path; no radio modification; open connector faces. |
| Formed tray or printed cradle | Plausible future alternatives, requiring their own bend/tooling or layer/creep/temperature qualification. They are not interchangeable with this machined geometry. |

P2 reduces the broad base12→8 mm, floor6→4, bars8→5, and upright wall/foot8→6. Four Ø12 bosses at X±68,Y−34.947/+41.053 rise toZ9.20 to preserve threads. The two bars remain160×20, separated76 mm alongY. Spacers are stock aluminum OD10/ID5.3/L30. Shims set actual fit instead of routinely facing every spacer. The upright members use two3 mm ribs: 8 mm rear returns and 8 mm foot ribs, continuous through the heel/root; R3 concave transitions add material. Each bracket is79×36×176, with a6 mm main web/foot and R6 front root. Two integral Ø20 foot-bolt seats rise toZ10.00±.10 (minimum9.90); their floor transitions receiveR0.2–0.5 added-material blends. Main sections stay thin while the bolt seats provide local bending depth. Thinning adds pocket machining and makes dimensional inspection more consequential; it does not imply a cheaper machining quote.

Flat gravity flows through four16×20 pan lands into the base and panel bolts. Upright loading flows through case stops/lands and retained bars, the base, four web bolts, two ribbed brackets and four foot bolts into the supporting plate. Side/end walls and spaced bars constrain translation/rotation. Retention uses positive geometry, with no friction credit. Films protect contact surfaces; neither film nor adhesive feet are structural supports. Cover/pan contact capacity remains a physical gate.

## Numerical design basis and checks

Assumptions: radio1.00kg; complete upright package2.20kg; g9.81m/s²; handling3g inertia plus1g gravity; cable disturbance20N at50mm (1N·m). Radio handling is59.24N, rounded60N. Package handling is106.33N, rounded110N horizontal and110N vertical concurrently plus1N·m. Local fabricated-member screen150N. Upright screen150N horizontal+150N vertical+1.5N·m, CG height≤120mm and X-offset≤50mm. These deliberately conservative component combinations are not a standard shock/vibration specification.

Certified unwelded6061-T6/T651 frame uses Sy240MPa,E70GPa, with yield SF target≥3 and adverse finished sections. Elementary beam/joint checks use bendingM/Z, elastic deflection, torsion, net-section/hole stress factors, fastener tension/shear, bearing, tear-out and female-thread shear area. The detailed reproducible calculations and source citations are in `references/engineering`. No FEA was used; case contact and joint behavior are better resolved by prototype inspection/testing than uncalibrated contact simulation.

The detailed results are conditional analytical screens, not a rated load claim. Principal outcomes: fabricated-frame minimum yield SF approximately3.02; upright predicted normal/handling/screen movement approximately0.266/0.863/1.185mm before joint/plate compliance. Bar deflection at the governing pad edge≈0.239mm plus≈0.020mm twist; floor≈0.061mm; deliberately conservative base rail≈0.235mm. These local movements, a0.30mm fitted gap and0.20mm film loss define upward motion bound1.056mm at one support, with the opposite support lowered0.20mm. The76mm station separation reduces rocking at the end stops. The calculation applies a further0.20mm dimensional reserve, leaving approximately0.15mm provisional end-capture margin; exact metal-pan-only obstruction witnesses are separately checked in `additional_verification.json`. Physical capture remains unvalidated.

Fasteners are McMaster Class12.9 black-oxide M5 cap screws, with Class10 zinc-plated nylon locknuts on through joints. Mixed joint capacities are not assigned the screw's class12.9 rating automatically. Female-thread screen requires≥7.5mm **complete** engagement, not gross entry. Nominal entry8.3492 and tip recess0.8508 for45mm screw,1mm washer,5mm bar,30mm spacer and0.6508mm selected spacer shims. Require≥0.5mm actual tip recess. Installation preload can dominate external demand: no torque value is invented. Qualify a controlled seating/locking procedure using actual hardware, coating and threadlocker; keep the radio gap open.

McMaster spacers specify aluminum but no alloy/temper/yield; hardness or tensile strength alone does not establish yield. No full-diameter load-spreading credit is taken for the purchased washer. The integral10mm seats are checked using8.0mm effective screw-head bearing width and a conservative net-strip model, with a factor3 at the boss transition. Verify the actual head bearing diameter≥8.0mm. The catalog BOM and calculation report specify receiving/prototype qualifications for these interfaces. Foot-top washers areØ15 for seating; M5×30 screws pass through the10mm local seats, with6.8mm nominal tail below the nut. Check OD≥14.9 and thickness≥1.0 as design acceptance limits, not published OD tolerance. Supporting-plate pullout, bending and its attachment to the box require actual plate material/span/edge distances. No threaded engagement into the supporting plate is credited; modeled attachment is through-bolted with underside nuts.

## Manufacture, fit and access

Use two-sided CNC milling of the base with fixture support during window/foot cuts; preserve4mm floor,5.5mm finished land elevation,7.8mm end-stop top and9.20mm boss top. Machine pockets/corner reliefs with6mm tooling; protect critical end capture edges (≤0.10mm edge break). Bars are simple profiled5mm stock with136mm hole pitch. Upright brackets are integral profiles from82×180×38.1mm rough stock: finish36width, retain the local Ø20 bolt seats toZ10, mill30mm-mouth channels with24mm flat floor betweenR3 fillets, drill orthogonal holes and preserve continuous ribs. Plan rigid workholding and tool reach; dimensions after finish. No bends, welds or sheet-metal flat apply. Deburr accessible edges0.2–0.5mm; black sulfuric anodize nominal10–15µm, masking threads/bearing seats. Use finish samples where appearance matters.

The nominal0.2508mm capture gap uses installed-film thickness0.20mm as a conservative CAD bound. Catalog PET8689K65 is0.127±0.0127mm and adhesive inclusion is unspecified. Measure total installed thickness,≤0.20, and select spacer shims to obtain0.20–0.30 at every pad cold and warm. Nominal CAD includes0.50+0.10+0.0508 under each spacer; actual shim count is as required. Film/spacer changes also change screw entry. See numerical tolerance stacks and reject any stack outside both gap and engagement/recess requirements. No forced fit or intentional cover preload.

Both end faces and K-slots remain accessible in nominal CAD. Assumed connector corridors extend60mm RF and70mm rear for plugs/bends; actual cable bodies, bend radii and tool motion must be verified. Use independent strain relief on the support;20N is not a connector allowable. Disconnect cables, support the upright radio, remove retainers/spacers/shims, then withdraw normal to the cradle. Assemble bracket feet to the plate before attaching the upright cradle. Case panels are serviced after radio removal. Anodize/film receive no safety-isolation, earth-bond or cooling credit. Verify enclosure airflow and thermal operation at representative TX/RX load in both orientations.
