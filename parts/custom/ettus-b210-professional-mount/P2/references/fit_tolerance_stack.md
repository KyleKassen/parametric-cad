Under-spacer shims change both top gap and screw entry. Additional under-head shims affect entry only. Every change must satisfy both limits; do not add a shim blindly. Nominal CAD rings are0.50,0.10 and0.0508 at each station; stock IDs/ODs vary within the catalog tolerances. Record installed combinations by station and reinstall matched sets. If no acceptable combination gives≥7.5mm complete engagement and≥.5mm recess, obtain the correct screw/stack and update the drawing—do not accept a protruding tip or clamp the radio.
