# P1 fit and hardware tolerance stacks

Units mm. These are worst-case arithmetic stacks, not statistical results. Drawing tolerances apply after finish. **The device allowances below are engineering assumptions requiring actual measurement; Ettus has not supplied these manufacturing tolerances.** Fit acceptance requires actual cold/warm gap setting and contact inspection.

| Stack | Nominal | Adverse allowances | Result / disposition |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Top capture gap `base thickness + spacer - upper film - land height - lower film - device height` | `12 +30 -.20 -7.50 -.20 -33.70 = .40` | Base ±.10; spacer ±.05; each film ±.03; land height ±.05; device height assumed ±.20; bar bearing-face flatness contribution ±.05 | Raw range **-.11 to +.91**. Nominal machining alone is not drop-in fit. Select spacer/shim stack to obtain measured .20-.40 at all four stations. |
| Lower side-pan clearance `pocket width - lower pan width` | `123.00 -121.84 =1.16` total | Pocket +.10/0; pan assumed ±.20 overall | Total .96-1.46, nominal .58 per side. Upper cover width122.348 is above the low side-stop contact band. Check actual band/seam geometry. |

Target .30 gap when selecting the first prototype's spacers. The raw vertical stack above can require roughly -.61 to +.41 change to the nominal30 spacer stack. Face spacers as matched pairs or select precision shims; record finished stack by position. **Under-bar shims change capture gap; under-head shims change screw engagement/tip recess without changing gap.** If shortening a spacer reduces screw-tip recess below .50, add a stainless under-head annular shim (ID5.5, OD≤10, appropriate thickness) and recheck≥8 completed threads. Do not force a longer screw into the support plate, bottom a screw, or close the device gap to compensate. Extra washers/shims are as-required BOM items; they are not all instantiated in the nominal assembly STEP.

The span of the raw gap range is why P1 requires a fit build. An interchangeability release across multiple radios needs measured device tolerances and either a revised fixed stack or a controlled spacer-selection schedule. Warm/cold checks cover differential expansion; no unverified enclosure alloy or thermal-expansion coefficient is assumed.
