All web sources below are manufacturer, material producer/supplier, fastener supplier, or fabrication-service publications. Source statements are paraphrased. Dimensions taken from the supplied STEP must be identified separately in the geometry inspection report.

## Correction of inherited local notes

The earlier vendor README and `parts/custom/ettus-b210-mount/DESIGN_NOTES.md` were reviewed for context, not accepted as requirements or certified manufacturer data. Two consequential corrections follow:

1. Small side slots should not be called verified vents. E1/E2 identify K-Slots for anti-theft use; correlate the exact STEP slots visually. Keep them clear, but do not engage them with locating tabs or infer a ventilation area/capacity.
2. The claim that PEM SOS is a blind-threaded type is incorrect. [PEM SOS-M3-10 product page](https://www.pemnet.com/products/product-finder/sos-m3-10/) explicitly identifies a through-hole M3 × 0.5 internal thread, 6H, 10 mm nominal length, 3.2 mm counterbore, and 300-series stainless material. This corrects nomenclature; it does not prove the installed component's identity or authorize external attachment. The listed performance tests use 1.5 mm 5052-H34 aluminum sheet, which does not establish capacity in the device's unverified steel pan. The geometric distance from a CAD bore entry to another modeled solid is an interference distance, not an approved screw penetration or safe engagement depth.

The new cradle should therefore avoid screws into the device, do not peel off feet by default, and avoid assigning structural capacity to adhesive feet or the unverified standoffs.

| ID | Source | Suitable preliminary input and limitation |

| M1 | [thyssenkrupp EN AW-6061 material data sheet, June 2018, pp. 2–3](https://d2zo35mdb530wx.cloudfront.net/_legacy/UCPthyssenkruppBAMXUK/assets.files/material-data-sheets/aluminium/aluminium-6061.pdf) | Sheet/plate T6/T62/T651, 0.4–100 mm thickness: listed minimum 0.2% yield 240 MPa, tensile 290 MPa. | M2 | [Hydro Alloy 6061 extrusion data sheet, 2019](https://www.hydro.com/globalassets/01-products--services/extruded-profiles/americas/ena-resources/alloy-data-sheets/hydro_2019_data_sheet_6061.pdf) | Independent producer cross-check for 6061 extruded T6/T6511 properties. Design interpretation: an unwelded bolted assembly of machined 6061 stock avoids a bend-radius/K-factor dependency and weld heat-affected strength reduction. Use actual available stock thicknesses in the CAD parameter file; face stock where a tighter finished thickness is needed. A clear or black anodized finish requires supplier agreement, allowance for coating, and a representative finish sample if color consistency matters. M1 cautions about decorative anodizing; do not promise uniform cosmetic color across mixed stock lots.

| H5 | [Henkel LOCTITE 243 technical data sheet, April 2025](https://datasheets.tdx.henkel.com/LOCTITE-243-en_GL.pdf) | Medium-strength anaerobic threadlocker for close-fitting metal threads and normal hand-tool removal. Cure depends on substrate, gap and temperature. Clean internal/external threads and dry; shake product; for through holes apply on the bolt at the engagement area, then assemble. Use an appropriate activator if cure is too slow. The sheet's 24 h at 23 °C is a stated condition for typical performance tests, not a universal cure guarantee. Published ISO 10964 breakaway/prevail torques characterize cured adhesive on particular test joints; they are not M5 installation tightening torques. Qualify cure and room-temperature removal on a representative joint before loading the mount. |
| H6 | [Henkel LOCTITE 243 product/application page](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/us/en/products/industrial-adhesives/central-pdp.html/loctite-243/BP000000316211/variation/1330906.html) | Describes primerless use on passive metals including stainless and aluminum. If cure is too slow on passive surfaces or below 5 °C, it identifies SF 7649 activator, subject to the TDS cure guidance. Fixture time is not a full-strength release criterion. |
| H7 | [Henkel anaerobic adhesive FAQ](https://next.henkel-adhesives.com/us/en/support/faqs/products-and-technologies/anaerobic-adhesives.html) | Specifically identifies stainless steel and anodized aluminum as inactive surfaces and recommends primers for many anaerobic products where both mating surfaces are inactive. Apply the product-specific H5/H6 guidance to 243 rather than imposing the generic rule unconditionally. The mount drawing masks tapped threads during anodizing; verify the actual thread finish and establish cure on that actual stainless/aluminum pair. |

Design interpretation: use a full-load-capability screw family such as ISO 4762 when relying on full tensile capacity. Button/low-head screw loadability may be reduced. Do not invent a torque from diameter alone: torque depends on joint preload need, strength, lubrication, finish, locking method, and female thread/seat capacity. Use positive enclosure stops and retainers so friction is not credited as the primary retention mechanism. Locknut engagement, thread protrusion, bolt length stack, washer support, tool corridor, and aluminum female-thread stripping need specific checks in the final engineering note. A generic 1D or 1.5D engagement rule alone is not a pullout calculation.

## Formed upright concept — research only, not the selected construction

The final upright support is machined 6061 with an 8 mm web/foot and R6 roots. The following 5052 and bending evidence documents an evaluated concept only; it does not specify the final BOM or require a bend coupon for the delivered machined adapter.

| ID | Primary source | Applicable evidence and limitation |
|---|---|---|
| M3 | [Aalco 5052-H32 sheet and treadplate data sheet](https://www.aalco.co.uk/datasheets/Aluminium-Alloy-5052-H32-Sheet-and-Treadplate_138.ashx) | The supplier's BS EN 485-2:2008 table covers 0.2–6.00 mm sheet/treadplate and lists minimum proof stress 130 MPa, tensile strength 210–260 MPa, modulus 70 GPa, and density 2.68 g/cm³. It explicitly describes its data as indicative; certify the actual alloy, temper, product form, thickness and mechanical properties. Do not substitute the often-quoted typical 193 MPa yield as a guaranteed minimum. This table covers 3.00 and 6.00 mm stock, but not 6.35 mm stock. |
| P4 | [Protocase bend radii and minimum bend sizes, pp. 1 and 5](https://www.protocase.com/resources/Protocase-Bend-Radii-Bend-Sizes.pdf) | Its actual tool/material combinations include 3.26 mm 5052-H32 with R3.175 mm and a 12.7 mm minimum bend dimension; 6.35 mm stock is listed with R9.525 mm and a 34.925 mm minimum bend dimension. The publication states that feasible radii and minimum bends depend on equipment/tooling. This supports roughly 1t radius feasibility for its approximately 3 mm stock and 1.5t for its approximately 6 mm stock. It does not guarantee exact R3 at 3.00 mm or R6 at 6.00 mm at the selected shop. |

Design interpretation for a future formed alternative: it would need bend strength, deflection and complete-assembly-mass checks, a cross-grain bend coupon from the actual stock with the actual punch/die, and a developed blank adjusted using measured radius/allowance and springback. A chosen K-factor would remain a flat-pattern assumption until that work was done. Maintain formed leg dimensions and hole-to-bend clearance if stock or tooling changes. These requirements do not apply to the selected machined support. Do not cold-bend the shared 6061-T6 cradle parts merely to unify the process.
