# Machined upright adapter — P1

Two identical 6061-T6 angles support the unchanged flat cradle in an upright orientation. The editable source is `vertical_adapter.py`, with dimensions in `adapter_params.json`. Each angle has 8 mm web and foot thickness, 36 mm finished width, a real machined R6 root, four Ø5.5 through holes, and R3 free corners. The footprint of the pair is 73 × 176 mm, and its height is 176 mm; this excludes installation screws and clearances. The complete assembly is larger where other hardware projects.

Each angle is one solid, cut from plate. Hold both web and foot thickness at 8.00 ±0.05 mm, minimum 7.95 mm after finish, because the minimum section enters the structural check. A suggested sawn blank is at least 75 × 180 × 38.1 mm, finished to 36 mm width. This uses significantly more stock than a bent bracket; it removes forming and bend-allowance uncertainties and uses the same machining route as the cradle. The shop may rough the open outline by waterjet, saw, or mill, then finish the side profile using a Ø12 endmill for the R6 root. Choose suitable flute length/reach for 36 mm profile depth, use restrained roughing passes and stable workholding, and finish the two broad faces. Drill the web and foot holes normal to their finished surfaces in two orthogonal setups. No inaccessible pocket, weld, sheet-metal bend, or five-axis feature is required.

Use certified stock and machine all dimensions after allowing for the chosen finish. Match the base finish: black sulfuric anodize nominal 10–15 µm; all drawing dimensions apply after finish. Deburr and break accessible edges 0.2–0.5 mm. Grounding is not established through anodized interfaces. The profile DXF is an XZ side outline, not a developed sheet-metal flat; its reference-hole lines and notes are not cuts.

Datums are A = mounting-foot underside (Z0), B = cradle mating face (X0), C = finished side face (Y−18). Hole centers lie 18 mm from C. Web holes are Z26 and Z166 from A, 140 mm apart. Foot holes are X20 and X55 from B, 35 mm apart. General dimensions ±0.2 mm; hole-center dimensions ±0.15 mm; holes Ø5.5 +0.1/−0. Hold A and B flat within 0.1 mm, and perpendicular within 0.1 mm over the web height. The final drawing has precedence over DXF text.

In assembly, locate bracket centers at Y−66.947 and Y+73.053. Rotate the cradle +90° about Y, then translate it Z96 mm. Its lower edge is Z16, giving 2 mm nominal clearance above the root at Z14. The web mates to the cradle underside; four through bolts at Z26/166 transfer load. The supporting plate sees load through two feet and four fasteners at X20/55 and the two Y stations. These brackets require bolting to a rigid support; they are not qualified as an unfastened desktop stand.

Install both feet on the supporting plate first. The inner foot screws at X20 are beneath the device after assembly and require this sequence. Set their 140 mm station pitch using the cradle as a positioning fixture before final tightening. Then fit the cradle using web through bolts and washers, with locking nuts as specified in the assembly BOM. Remove the cradle from the brackets before accessing hidden foot fasteners. Keep the outer X55 access corridor and the rear-facing web bolt tool corridor clear inside the installation box. No support-plate thickness, thread capacity, electrical bond, or thermal enclosure performance is established by this part model.
