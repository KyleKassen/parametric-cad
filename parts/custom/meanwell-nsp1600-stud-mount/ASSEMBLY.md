# Mean Well NSP-1600 adapter — N1 assembly

**Preliminary instructions; no physical assembly or test has been performed.** Use with the final drawings, [BOM](BOM.md), [engineering note](references/engineering/engineering_checks.md) and [prototype plan](PROTOTYPE_VALIDATION.md). The working installation is horizontal, bottom down, indoors on a rigid panel. The 12 V supply is provisional; confirm the received variant.

## Inspect before installation

1. Disconnect and secure all power sources against reconnection, remove cables as required, and follow the manufacturer's service instructions. Preserve the supply cover, original hardware and designated FG protective-earth connection. The adapter is not an independently qualified earth bond.
2. Check the actual three bottom M3 hole positions against the 1:1 template: X±35/Y−16.1 and X0/Y−280.8 mm. Manufacturer general dimensional tolerance is ±0.5 mm; three conical seats do not guarantee a drop-in fit. If the screws will not start freely together, revise the CAD coordinates and remake the affected features. Do not force screws, elongate countersinks, drill the supply or use nearby cover holes.
3. Inspect the finished 125×300.6×5.00±0.05 mm plate. Its two upper fan reliefs are 0.50±0.05 mm deep, bounded by X−40..−2.85 and X2.85..40, Y−299.1..−269.5 mm. Keep the 5.7 mm central metal contact land and outside lands intact. Remove pocket burrs without a large mouth chamfer. The specified clear chemical conversion finish follows machining; dimensions apply after finish.
4. Gauge each received M3×8 screw in its finished underside seat. The head must seat fully without rocking and finish flush to 0.10 mm below datum A. Measure all three projections: target 2.75–3.35 mm from the upper plate seating face; **never exceed Mean Well's 4.00 mm maximum from the PSU bottom**. Confirm complete male threads traverse the actual short flanged boss and clamp-up occurs without internal obstruction. CAD does not establish boss strength or complete engagement.

## Assemble on the bench, then install on the panel

1. Support the disconnected supply on a suitable case-safe fixture that leaves the bottom accessible; do not rest it on terminals, fans or exposed features. Place the adapter with fan pockets facing the supply. Start all three [91294A128 M3×8 screws](https://www.mcmaster.com/91294A128/) by hand, without washers under their heads.
2. Tighten evenly using the qualified joint procedure. Mean Well recommends 6–8 kgf·cm (0.5884–0.7845 N·m) for its bottom mounts. This does not by itself qualify this countersunk head, aluminum seat, finish or locking treatment. Resolve that compatibility before production tightening; no threadlocker or alternate torque is specified here. Confirm full metal seating and **at least 0.20 mm actual clearance to both plastic fan frames** under normal service. Plastic must not carry clamp load.
3. Inspect the four factory-installed FPE WGU30 M3×12 load studs at X±55/Y−16.1 and −280.8 mm. Confirm actual pattern, projection, cure/installation acceptance, flush bases and clean flat panel seating. The 145×340×6 mm panel is an interface reference only; final panel support and anchor qualification remain open. Do not grind installed studs or add a pad/TIM under the adapter.
4. Lower the complete supply/adapter over the studs without binding. Fit one [98688A142 washer](https://www.mcmaster.com/98688A142/) and one [90576A102 locknut](https://www.mcmaster.com/90576A102/) per stud. Nominal tail beyond each nut is 2.45 mm; verify complete nylon engagement and full exposed threads. Use an actual 5.5 mm AF socket, OD≤10 mm and internal depth≥10 mm. Tightening and prevailing torque must be compatible with the qualified FPE anchor; no unsupported torque is assigned. Apply inspection witness marks after accepted tightening.
5. Verify the final cables, lugs, covers, FG conductor, strain relief and tools. Preserve factory intake at the terminal end and fan exhaust at the opposite end. The 100 mm end corridors are project layout reserves, not a universal manufacturer clearance specification. Maintain the manufacturer's adjacent-heat-source clearance and applicable derating. No heat rejection through this adapter is credited.

## Removal and reinstallation

Disconnect and secure the supply against energization, disconnect and support cables, then support the unit while removing four nuts and washers. Lift the complete supply/adapter at least 20 mm to clear the studs. The three countersunk screws are accessible only after removal to the bench. Inspect seats, threads, finish, fan gaps and witness marks at each reinstallation. Evaluate locknut retention during the proposed ten prototype service cycles; replace nuts under the qualified final procedure. The nylon limit is 85°C at the actual nut, not a system ambient rating.
