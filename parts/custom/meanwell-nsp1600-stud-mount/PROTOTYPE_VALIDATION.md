# Mean Well NSP-1600 adapter — N1 prototype validation

**Proposed work, not performed.** CAD/manufacturing checks demonstrate nominal geometry and file consistency. They do not qualify the PSU mounting bosses, countersunk heads, FPE anchors, installation preload, final panel or powered enclosure. Record part/PSU serial numbers, drawing revision, material certificate, hardware lots, instruments, fixture compliance, temperatures, loads and photographs. A failed criterion requires correction and repeat of the affected checks.

## 1. Received-device and manufactured fit

With the supply disconnected and its cover intact, confirm the voltage variant, case revision, 1.8 kg manufacturer mass and actual complete-package mass. The mechanical calculation allows 2.4 kg for the complete package. Establish the bottom metal seating plane, terminal plane and width center. Measure the three authorized M3 axes, fan outline, central metal spine, chassis seating contacts and accessible thread geometry. Compare with the 1:1 template before final drilling/countersinking; nominal fixed cones cannot absorb the manufacturer's general ±0.5 mm tolerance. Do not use forced assembly to compensate for pattern error.

Inspect the finished plate thickness 5.00±0.05 mm; both 0.50±0.05 mm relief depths; pocket limits X−40..−2.85/X2.85..40, Y−299.1..−269.5 mm; intact 5.7 mm central land; clean seating faces; and drawing flatness/location controls. Check actual lateral fan clearance: the nominal 0.10 mm margin beside the modeled central bracket is especially sensitive to device variation and pocket placement. Confirm clear chemical conversion finish, with no raised burr, chip or coating build holding either seating plane apart.

Gauge all three received screws and seats. Proposed project criteria are a head flush to 0.10 mm recessed, projection 2.75–3.35 mm, **absolute manufacturer maximum 4.00 mm**, Ø3.3–3.5 mm throat, countersink mouth≤6.2 mm, nominal 90° included angle with minimum 89°, upper deburr≤0.10 mm, straight cylindrical ligament≥3.30 mm and effective conical seat diameter≥5.7 mm. These seat criteria are analytical project inputs, not Mean Well specifications. Verify full seat contact, actual boss thread traversal and unobstructed clamp-up; do not infer thread pullout capacity or a minimum complete female engagement from the simplified STEP.

Inspect the actual FPE panel, installed studs, location, flushness, projection, anchoring process and support. After provisional bench assembly, require full metal seating, no proud screw heads and **≥0.20 mm measured fan-frame gap under normal service**. Use a feeler/witness method that cannot enter the fan or damage it. Check four washer/nut stacks, full nylon engagement, stud tails and actual socket clearance. No TIM is used.

## 2. Joint qualification and surrogate load testing

Resolve the manufacturer mounting-torque compatibility with the selected DIN 7991 screw, finished cone and short case boss. Qualify representative joints before applying production torque or a proof load to a real supply. The published 0.5884–0.7845 N·m range is not a measured clamp force or countersunk-head capacity. Test and document the accepted locking method and repeated tightening; no threadlocker is specified by default. Separately qualify the actual FPE anchor, panel thickness, cure, temperature and allowable installation/prevailing torque.

Use a metal surrogate reproducing the three attachment positions and metal support lands, plus a representative supported panel/stud coupon, for initial destructive-limit or proof work. Apply load through this fixture, not connectors, fan frames, covers or a live PSU. Remove fixture compliance from reported displacement. The preliminary load envelope is gravity plus 3g handling, a 50 N cable force and a separate 5 N·m cable couple: 125 N device resultant and 145 N complete-package resultant. These are force resultants with direction varied between cases, not full simultaneous loading on all three axes.

After all joints and fixtures are qualified, a separately reviewed 1.5× whole-assembly proof may use the corresponding 187.5 N device /217.5 N package envelope and 7.5 N·m couple. These are alternative interface force descriptions, not additional forces to sum together. Define a fixture/load schedule that reproduces the selected case without double-counting cable forces or damaging the supply. Hold each stable static plateau for 60 seconds, record displacement during load and after unloading, and inspect for loosening or damage. No actual-unit proof test is authorized by an unverified analytical factor alone. Maintain ≥0.20 mm fan gap in normal service and no plastic contact under the qualified handling cases.

## 3. Service, cable access and thermal operation

Perform ten unpowered removal/reinstallation cycles using the complete adapter/supply assembly. Confirm the ≥20 mm lift path, underside screw access on the bench, nut/socket access and intact threads/seats. Record witness-mark movement, prevailing torque under the approved procedure, fan gap and seating after cycles 1, 5 and 10. Replace final locknuts as the qualified procedure requires. Fit actual lugs, insulation barriers, cables and strain relief; use cable-maker bend radii and verify terminal screwdriver access. Apply cable-handling loads through the strain-relief fixture, never unqualified live terminals.

Qualified electrical personnel should test the final powered enclosure using the confirmed voltage variant, intended input range/load duty and maximum intended inlet ambient. Start with monitored moderate load, then progress to the intended operating extremes after fit and electrical checks pass. Log supply inlet/exhaust, enclosure, panel/nut and TEC hot-side temperatures, humidity, input/output power and fan operation until stable; a proposed stabilization criterion is less than 1°C change over 15 minutes after at least one hour at the test point. Extend testing for cycling or continued drift. Verify applicable Mean Well horizontal/low-input derating, unobstructed intake/exhaust and absence of harmful recirculation or condensation. Stay within the actual nut's 85°C catalog limit.

At the provisional 12 V full-load point, 1500 W output and 89% typical efficiency imply about 185 W PSU loss. This is a planning calculation, not worst-case measured heat or demonstrated TEC capacity. Include all other internal dissipation and the complete TEC heat-rejection path. Accept thermal operation only against confirmed manufacturer limits and the final enclosure requirements; no universal allowable housing temperature was established. Repeat fit/gap/locking inspection hot and after cooling.

Record unresolved load, ambient, wiring, panel or mounting-orientation requirements as release holds. The prototype is not vehicle, airborne, outdoor, overhead, seismic or vibration-qualified. Changed orientation, support, mass, cable loads or duty requires review and proportionate repeat testing.
